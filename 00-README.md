The ideas were first written down between March and July 2026, as the manifest shows; this write-up was first published on the date in its first line, before it was used in any engagement, so that both dates can be checked by anyone.

## How to cite

An idea from the snapshot is cited by its id, for example "the definition of done for an encoded asset (BG-11)". The manifest is the record of what that id rests on. A deliverable that builds on an idea says so in its own words and cites the id; it does not copy the section.

## The boundary, in both directions

These files are the author's. The ideas in them predate the engagements they are used in, and the files were published before they were used in any. They carry no material belonging to any company the author works with, and nothing from them should be presented as created for one. In the other direction, a company's own methods, documents, customers and figures belong in that company's tools and never in a later version of this package. `03-project-instructions.md` holds the working rules.
